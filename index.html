Thank You Note

A Parting Letter

"I wanted to tell you this, even though I’m not sure if it’s the right or wrong thing to do—I’ve just been feeling exhausted.

Still, if you ever reach out, I will always be here for you. Anytime, anywhere, whenever you feel comfortable to message.

I also realize that whatever I'm saying today comes from wondering whether what I feel truly aligns with what you feel. I respect your space and your choices, and I know you are completely free to decide what’s best for you.

But I want you to know one thing: I will never forget you. You will always hold a special place in my heart, filled with memories and moments I truly cherish.

I’m sorry for everything, and I hope you can forgive me. I don’t know how you feel right now, but I genuinely wish you all the very best in your life ahead.

Thank you so much, always."
